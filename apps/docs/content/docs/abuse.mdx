---
title: Report abuse and appeal decisions
description: Report a paste without an account and operate the restricted manual review queue on your own instance.
---

An instance can accept abuse reports and appeals at `/abuse`. No account is required.
The operator must enable this feature; it is off by default.

## Send a report

1. Open **Report abuse** at the bottom-right of a shared paste page, or open `/abuse` on the instance.
2. Fill in **Paste URL on this instance**, choose a **Reason**, and describe your concern in
   **Explanation (10 to 2,000 characters)**.
3. Optionally enter an **Email (optional)** address so a reviewer can contact you.
4. Select **Send for review**. A confirmation means the report was stored for review.

For suspected child exploitation, provide its location only. Do not download, copy, quote or
upload suspected material. The form accepts no attachments. If a child is in immediate danger,
contact local emergency services.

To challenge a moderation decision, select **Appeal a moderation decision** and explain why you
believe it should be reconsidered. An appeal does not automatically restore access. Decision
notices and email replies are handled manually by the operator; this feature sends no emails.

The form accepts URLs on the configured app or share origin. Query parameters and fragments are
discarded because they can carry passwords or encryption keys. Do not put paste contents or
credentials in the explanation. Reports about missing, private and restricted pastes receive the
same confirmation, so reporting does not reveal whether a paste exists.

## Understand report handling

Reports stay in the instance's database for authorized host operators. They are not forwarded to
Linear, an AI provider or a public issue tracker. Reviewers see report text only when they
explicitly open a case. Report text is untrusted and may itself contain sensitive material.

The instance accepts at most 10 submissions per address per hour. Duplicate submissions with
the same normalized fields on the same UTC day share one case. Neither the case ID nor duplicate
status is exposed publicly. Report counts do not change moderation state.

If the form says too many reports were sent, wait an hour before retrying. A failed submission
does not confirm receipt; check the fields and retry. A request body over 12 KiB is refused.

## Enable manual review

Before enabling intake, assign a primary reviewer and backup, confirm reporting and preservation
requirements, and establish a restricted case register, notice procedure and retention/disposal
schedule. Test the queue and the incident escalation with harmless synthetic reports.

Set `ABUSE_REPORTS_ENABLED=true`, `BETTER_AUTH_URL` and `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET` (at least 32
characters), then restart every serving replica. Confirm `/abuse` loads in a browser (it returns
`404` when `ABUSE_REPORTS_ENABLED=false`). Configure `CLIENT_IP_SOURCE` for the trusted proxy;
otherwise visitors can share a quota. See [Configuration](/self-hosting/configuration).

From an authorized operations checkout with `DATABASE_URL` pointing at the intended database:

```sh
bun --filter @workspace/db moderation reports
bun --filter @workspace/db moderation report <report-uuid>
```

Both commands print JSON to standard output (or exit with status `1` on error). The queue lists at
most 100 unresolved cases, with suspected child exploitation first and then oldest first. It shows
only the case UUID, category and receipt time. Opening a case shows its location, immutable paste
UUID when known, explanation, optional contact and disposition. These commands audit the host
username, hostname, action, case UUID when applicable, and time. They require trusted host and
database access; there is no public queue or browser admin endpoint.

## Record a decision

Inspect the paste's current content and moderation versions, apply a restriction or release with
`caseReference` set to the report UUID, and then close the report with the transition's command
UUID:

```sh
bun --filter @workspace/db moderation inspect <paste-uuid>
bun --filter @workspace/db moderation transition '{
  "commandId": "22222222-2222-4222-8222-222222222222",
  "pasteId": "<paste-uuid>",
  "expectedContentVersion": 1,
  "expectedModerationVersion": 0,
  "state": "quarantined",
  "reasonCode": "policy_violation",
  "caseReference": "11111111-1111-4111-8111-111111111111"
}'
bun --filter @workspace/db moderation resolve-report '{
  "id": "11111111-1111-4111-8111-111111111111",
  "outcome": "quarantined",
  "decisionCommandId": "22222222-2222-4222-8222-222222222222"
}'
```

Each command prints the resulting JSON record on success (or exits with status `1` if a version
check or field validation fails). The UUIDs above are synthetic: replace them with the actual case
UUID, paste UUID, version numbers from `inspect`, and a fresh command UUID.
`quarantined`, `removed` and `restored` outcomes require an audit event for that case and the
same immutable paste, with the matching resulting state. `restored` matches `allowed`.
`no_action` and `duplicate` close a case without a moderation command. Resolution is audited and
cannot be applied twice. It records a completed decision; it does not itself change paste state.

Use the confirmed incident procedure for urgent allegations. Keep suspected material out of
ordinary logs, email, chat and issue trackers. Send any permitted decision notice through the
operator's approved channel. A restriction alone does not preserve a paste: expiry, the trash and
account deletion still delete it. Place a legal hold when the paste must be kept.

## Preserve a paste

A legal hold keeps a quarantined or removed paste, with its files and revisions, until you release
it. While it is held, the database refuses to delete the paste by any path, including expiry,
burn after read, the trash, the purge job and account deletion. Its owner sees "This account
can't be deleted right now" when they try to delete their account. A held paste can't be released
to `allowed` or `pending`, so neither its owner nor anyone else can open or edit it.

```sh
bun --filter @workspace/db moderation hold '{
  "pasteId": "<paste-uuid>",
  "caseReference": "11111111-1111-4111-8111-111111111111"
}'
bun --filter @workspace/db moderation release-hold '{
  "pasteId": "<paste-uuid>",
  "caseReference": "11111111-1111-4111-8111-111111111111"
}'
```

`hold` refuses a paste that is `allowed` or `pending`, so quarantine or remove it first. Placing
the same hold twice changes nothing, and a paste can be held for one case at a time.
`release-hold` needs the case UUID the hold was placed with. After the release, the paste follows
its normal lifecycle again, so a paste that expired or sat in the trash for 30 days is deleted by
the next purge. `inspect` shows `legalHoldCase` and `legalHoldAt`.

A hold covers the live database only. Backups still age out on their own schedule.

## Suspend an account

A suspension signs the account out everywhere, refuses new sign-ins and stops its API tokens and
OAuth grants until you reinstate it. It doesn't change the account's pastes, so restrict them with
`transition` as well.

```sh
bun --filter @workspace/db moderation suspend '{
  "userId": "<account-id>",
  "caseReference": "11111111-1111-4111-8111-111111111111"
}'
bun --filter @workspace/db moderation reinstate '{
  "userId": "<account-id>",
  "caseReference": "11111111-1111-4111-8111-111111111111"
}'
```

The account's owner sees "This account is suspended" when they sign in with a password. Its tokens
answer `401` as if they were revoked. Reinstating restores sign-in and every token that hasn't
expired. Suspending a suspended account changes nothing.

Holds and suspensions are recorded with the host username, hostname, case UUID and time. The
record holds no paste content, survives the deletion of the paste and the account, and can't be
edited.

## Retention and limitations

Report records and reviewer audit events survive paste and account deletion. There is no automatic
report-disposal job. Keep intake off until an authorized owner has approved the retention and
disposal procedure, including backups and review access. The report queue is not an evidence
repository.

Setting `ABUSE_REPORTS_ENABLED=false` hides the form and report links and rejects new submissions
(`/abuse` returns `404`). Existing cases remain available through the host commands. Disabling
intake does not dispose of records or change paste restrictions.

This workflow provides intake, an audited host queue and manual case resolution. It does not
include account suspension, automated decision notices, verified preservation, external escalation
delivery, automated screening or backlog alerts. No claim of complete detection or legal
certification is made.
