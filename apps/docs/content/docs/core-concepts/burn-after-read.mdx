---
title: Burn after read
description: Share a Sniptide paste that one person can read once, then it moves to your trash. Learn what readers, link previews and you see.
---

**Burn after reading** makes a paste readable once. The first person who opens it and confirms
gets the code, and then the paste is gone from its link. Use it for something that should be read
once, such as a one-off credential.

Turn it on in the editor's **Protection** setting, or make it your default under
[paste defaults](/settings#set-paste-defaults).

![A sealed link leads to one confirmed read, after which the paste moves to trash.](/visuals/burn-after-read.svg)

## What a reader sees

Opening the link doesn't burn the paste. The reader first sees a sealed page that says the paste
can be read once, with an **Open and burn** button. Only that button reveals the code and burns
the paste. After that, the code stays on the page until the reader leaves it, so they should copy
what they need straight away.

The sealed page makes the link safe to post in a chat. Link previews, mail scanners, search
crawlers and browser prefetches only load the sealed page, so they can't use up the read.

## What you see as the owner

Your own paste's link behaves the same for you. Opening it shows the sealed page with a note that
opening it there deletes it too. To look at the paste without burning it, open it from your
pastes in the app instead.

## What happens after the read

- **One reader gets it.** If two people select **Open and burn** at the same moment, one gets the
  code and the other sees that the paste is gone.
- **It moves to your trash.** The link stops working at once, and **Trash** shows when it was
  read. After 30 days it is deleted.
- **Restore brings it back for you only.** A restored paste is private and burn after read is
  off, so its link never opens a second time. Change its visibility if you want to share it again.
- **You get a notification**, and an email if you turned those on, unless you burned it yourself.

## Restrictions

- Only you can read the paste's raw files or print it.
- It can't be embedded, posted to Slack or Discord, or shown in a link preview.
- It isn't listed under **Shared with me** for people you invited. They read it once through its
  link, like anyone else.

## Combine it with other settings

A burn-after-read paste can also have a password, which the reader enters before the sealed page
appears, and an expiry. A paste that expires before anyone reads it is removed like any other
expired paste.
