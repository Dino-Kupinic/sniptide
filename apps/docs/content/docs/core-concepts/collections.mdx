title: Collections

A collection is a named group of your pastes, shown in the sidebar. It is how you keep, say, all
your API snippets or one project's scripts together.

## Using collections

- **New collection** in the sidebar asks for a name. Names can be up to 40 characters, and an
  account can have up to 50 collections.
- File a paste by choosing a collection in its **Collection** field, or from its **⋯** menu with
  **Add to collection**. A paste belongs to at most one collection. Choose **No collection** to
  take it out.
- Each collection gets an icon and a colour, which you can change. Drag collections in the sidebar
  to reorder them.
- The collection's page (`/collections/<name>`) lists its pastes. `G` then `C` opens the list of
  all your collections.

Renaming a collection changes only its name. Its link stays as it was made, and its pastes stay
in it. **Delete collection** removes the collection, not its pastes: they are kept and simply leave
the collection.

## Collections are private

A collection is an organizer for **you**. Other people can't open it, and sharing a collection
isn't something Sniptide does: you share the **pastes** in it, each with its own
[sharing settings](/core-concepts/sharing). To hand someone several related snippets at once,
put them in one paste as separate files (up to 10), or invite them to each paste.

## Collections as ordered sets of snippets

Because a collection is just the pastes filed under it, the [CLI](/cli) and the
[MCP server](/mcp) can work with it as a whole: they can list a collection, and pull it down as
one ordered set of pastes, oldest capture first. That is useful for a repeatable workflow, such as
a set of setup snippets you restore in order into a new project.
