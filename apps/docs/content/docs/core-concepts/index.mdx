## How it fits together

- A **paste** is a title, an optional description and up to 10 files, with a link of its own.
- You file pastes in **collections** to find them again. Collections belong to you alone.
- Each paste has its own **sharing** settings: who can open the link, whether it needs a password,
  which accounts you invited, and when it expires.
- Editing a paste keeps a history of **revisions** you can roll back to.
- A deleted paste goes to the **trash** for 30 days before it is gone for good.

Everything is per account. Your pastes, collections, starred pastes and settings are yours; other
people only ever see a paste you shared with them.
