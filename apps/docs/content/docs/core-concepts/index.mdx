Sniptide has one main object, the **paste**, and a few ways to organize, share and track it. This
section explains each one and where its setting lives.

<HowItFitsVisual />

## How the pieces relate

- A **paste** is a title, an optional description and 1 to 10 files, reached through its own link.
- You file pastes in **collections** to find them again. A collection belongs to you alone.
- Each paste has its own **sharing** settings: who can open the link, an optional password, the
  people you invited, and when it expires. **Burn after read** makes a paste readable once.
- Editing a paste records **revisions** that you can compare and restore.
- A deleted paste stays in the **trash** for 30 days before it is removed for good.
- **Encrypted secrets** hold a project's environment variables, separate from pastes.
- **Analytics** counts views, copies and referrers for your pastes in your own database.

Everything belongs to one account. Other people only see a paste you shared with them, and a token
or agent connection only reaches the pastes of the account that created it.
