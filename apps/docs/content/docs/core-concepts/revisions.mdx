---
title: Revisions
description: Every paste keeps a history of its edits, and you can roll back.
---

When you edit a paste, Sniptide keeps a **revision**: a saved copy of its title, description and
files. Open **Revisions** on a paste to see the history.

## How revisions are recorded

Auto-save would fill a history with one entry per pause in typing, so edits are batched. While you
keep editing, changes fold into the newest revision, as long as it is less than 10 minutes old and
you haven't closed the editor with **Done**. After **Done**, or after 10 minutes, the next edit
starts a new revision. An edit that rewrites most of a paste always gets a revision of its own.

Creating a paste and importing a gist also record a first revision.

## Rolling back

**Restore** on a revision puts the paste's title, description and files back the way that
revision left them. It does not touch the sharing settings, the link or the collection. The
rollback is a revision itself ("Restored revision N"), so the version you replaced is still in the
history and you can restore it in turn. Nothing is lost by restoring.

People you gave **Edit** access to can restore revisions too. The history is shown 20 entries at
a time.

## What is kept

Sniptide keeps the full copy for the **20 newest** revisions of a paste. Older ones stay in the
history as a log entry but can no longer be restored. History from before copies were kept has
no copy either; those entries are marked as not restorable.
