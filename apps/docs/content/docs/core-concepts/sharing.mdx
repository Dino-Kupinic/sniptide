---
title: Sharing
description: Who can open a paste, how to protect it, and when it goes away.
---

Every paste has its own sharing settings. Change them in the editor under **Visibility & expiry**,
or later with **Share** on the paste.

## Visibility

| Visibility | Who can open it |
| --- | --- |
| **Private** | You and the accounts you invited. Everyone else gets a 404, as if the link didn't exist. |
| **Unlisted** | Anyone with the link. Search engines are told not to index it. |
| **Public** | Anyone with the link, and search engines may index it. Public pastes without a password or burn setting also get a link preview (see below). |

Unlisted is the default for new pastes. "Unlisted" means the link is the secret: don't treat it as
access control for anything sensitive.

## Password

Set a **Paste password** on a public or unlisted paste and visitors must enter it before they can
see the code. The password is stored only as a salted hash. Change or remove it from **Share**.
You and the people you invited never see the prompt. Too many wrong guesses from one address are
rate-limited.

## Invite people

For a private paste (or any paste), **Invite people** with a Sniptide account, by email or
username, and choose their access:

- **View** lets them read it.
- **Edit** also lets them change the title, description and files. The link, visibility, expiry,
  password, collection and burn setting stay yours.

They find it under **Shared with me** as soon as you invite them, and you can remove access any
time. They can also remove it from their own list, and you can invite them again. You can invite
people when you create a paste, too.

## Raw access

Each file is available as plain text at `<domain>/<link>/raw?file=<file name>`, for `curl` and
scripts. **Allow raw access** turns that off for everyone but you. It follows the other settings:
a private paste, a locked one and an expired one aren't readable through raw either, and a
[burn-after-read](/core-concepts/burn-after-read) paste has no raw access for anyone but you.

## Expiry

Choose **Expires after**: 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, 1 month or never. When it passes, the link stops
working for everyone, and the paste is removed after another 30 days. Sniptide notifies
you about pastes that are about to expire.

## Link previews and channels

Public pastes without a password or one-time protection give link previews (Open Graph and X
cards) with the title, language and the first ten lines of the first file. Previews and other
automated fetches never count as a view and never use up a burn-after-read paste.

If the administrator configured Slack or Discord webhooks, **Share → Share to Slack / Discord**
posts the link to the channel. It is only offered for public or unlisted pastes with no password
or one-time protection, and everyone in the channel receives the link.

## Views

A paste's page shows how many times it was opened. Only people opening the share page count:
you don't, and neither do crawlers, link previews or scripts.
