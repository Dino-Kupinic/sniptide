---
title: Your first paste
description: From sign-up to a shared paste in a collection, step by step.
---

This walkthrough goes from a new account to code you have filed in a collection and shared. On a
self-hosted instance, open your own address instead of app.sniptide.com; everything else is the
same.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create an account

Open the app and choose **Create account**. You pick a username (3 to 30 lowercase letters, digits,
hyphens or underscores), enter an email and set a password of at least 8 characters. If the
instance has GitHub or Google sign-in turned on you can use those instead.

Some instances close or limit sign-up: an invite-only instance only accepts the emails its
administrator allowed, and a closed one has no sign-up at all. The sign-up page says which.

</Step>
<Step>

### Make a paste

Choose **New paste** in the sidebar, or press `N` anywhere in the app. Give it a title, add one or
more files (a paste holds up to 10) and type or paste your code. File names matter: the language,
and so the syntax highlighting, comes from the extension.

Below the editor, **Visibility & expiry** has the settings that decide who can open the paste and
for how long. A new paste starts as **Unlisted** and expires after **1 week**; you can change those
defaults under Settings, and change them again per paste. [Sharing](/core-concepts/sharing)
explains every option.

Press `Ctrl+Enter` (`⌘ Enter` on a Mac) to create the paste.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create a collection and file the paste

Collections are your own folders for pastes. Choose **New collection** in the sidebar, give it a
name, and create it. Then open the paste's **⋯** menu and choose **Add to collection**, or pick the
collection in the paste's **Collection** field.

The collection appears in the sidebar with its pastes. See [Collections](/core-concepts/collections).

</Step>
<Step>

### Share it

Open the paste and choose **Share**. **Copy link** gives you the paste's address
(`<domain>/<link>`), which anyone can open while the paste is public or unlisted. To share with
specific people instead, set the paste to **Private** and **Invite** them by email or username:
they get it under **Shared with me**, with view or edit access, and everyone else gets a 404.

A collection is private to you, so you share the pastes in it, not the collection itself.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What next

- A paste's share page shows how many times it was opened.
- Press `?` to see every keyboard shortcut, or `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` to search pastes and collections.
- Deleted pastes stay in **Trash** for 30 days, so a slip is recoverable.
- To capture code from a project, see the [CLI](/cli). To let an AI assistant use your pastes, see
  the [MCP server](/mcp).
