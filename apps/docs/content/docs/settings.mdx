title: Account settings
description: Change your Sniptide profile, sign-in methods, paste defaults, notifications, language, API tokens and see your usage.
---

Open **Settings** from the account menu, or press `⌘,` (`Ctrl+,`). On a phone, **Settings** opens
a list of the sections below.

| Section | What you change there |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Profile, language and editor |
| **Account** | Email, password, connected GitHub and Google accounts, signed-in devices, account deletion |
| **Paste defaults** | Visibility, expiry and burn after read for new pastes |
| **Notifications** | In-app and email notifications per event |
| **API tokens** | Tokens for the CLI, SDKs and agents, and agent connections |
| **Usage** | Storage, paste count and API requests |

## Edit your profile and editor

**General** holds your display name, username and avatar, which appear on your public pastes and
share pages. It also holds the editor's look: indentation (2 spaces, 4 spaces or a tab), line
numbers and **Secret detection**, which warns when code looks like a key or token.

The theme follows your system until you change it, and the choice is saved on this device.

## Choose a language

The app is available in English, German, Spanish, Japanese and Simplified Chinese. On your first
visit it uses your browser's preferred language. Choose another one in **General**, on the
sign-in page or in the account menu. The choice is saved for this browser. Share pages have their
own language selector. Paste contents, file names and programming language names stay as they
are.

## Manage sign-in and devices

**Account** lets you:

- Change your email. If the instance sends email and your address is verified, the change takes
  effect after the old address approves it and you verify the new one. Without email delivery, it
  takes effect at once after you confirm your current password.
- Change your password, or add one to an account that signs in with GitHub or Google. Select
  **Sign out on my other devices** to end every other session at the same time.
- Connect or disconnect GitHub and Google sign-in.
- See your signed-in devices and sign out any you don't recognize.
- Delete your account. This deletes your sessions and pastes for good, and their share links stop
  working.

If you forget your password, select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. A reset link works once,
for one hour, and an account can request three an hour. Using one signs the account out
everywhere. Password resets need the instance to send email. Without it, the page asks you to
contact the administrator.

## Set paste defaults

**Paste defaults** decides how **New paste**, **Quick paste** and gist imports start. The defaults
are **Unlisted**, **1 week** and burn after read off. You can still change each paste. See
[Sharing](/core-concepts/sharing) for what each option does.

## Choose notifications

**Notifications** turns in-app and email delivery on or off for each event:

| Event | When it fires |
| --- | --- |
| **Shared with you** | Someone invites you to a paste. |
| **Burn after read** | Your paste is read and moved to the trash. |
| **Expiring soon** | Your paste will expire within a day. |
| **View milestones** | Your paste reaches a view milestone. |

In-app notifications are on and email is off by default. To turn on email, the instance must send
email and you must verify your address: select **Send verification email** in this section. You
can request three verification emails an hour. Each notification email has a link that stops
emails for that event only.

## Create API tokens

**API tokens** creates the tokens that the CLI, the SDKs and agents use, and lists agents you
connected with OAuth. See [API tokens](/api-tokens) and [OAuth for agents](/oauth).

## Check your usage

**Usage** shows your storage and paste count against the account limits, with a warning when you
approach them. Both include pastes in the trash. It also charts the requests your API tokens made
each day over the last 30 days, in UTC. The token API returns the same data at
[`GET /api/v1/usage`](/api-tokens#endpoints).
