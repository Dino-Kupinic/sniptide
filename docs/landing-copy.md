## Pillars

### Paste and share
Capture a snippet or a whole folder and get a short link. Keep it private, share it unlisted, or
go public. Add a password, an expiry or burn-after-read when the link needs to disappear.

### Built for agents
Turn your best code into patterns Claude Code and Cursor can find. Agents search, read and follow
your collections over MCP, and the same library works from the CLI.

### Secrets, handled
Sync your `.env` files as encrypted env groups. Pull them into a project or run a command with
them set, and values never show up in tool output.

## Self-hosting

Your pastes, your server. One Docker image and Postgres, open source under AGPL-3.0. Updates are
`docker compose pull`.

## How it compares

Not a feature grid: we can't vouch for other products' current features, so the page says what we
do and where we fit.

- **Instead of a gist:** the same quick link, on your server, with passwords, expiry and
  burn-after-read.
- **Instead of a pastebin:** open source, with accounts, revisions and tools for your agents.
- **Next to Infisical:** Infisical is a full secrets platform. Reach for it when you need
  rotation, audit and access policies. Sniptide covers the everyday case: a project's `.env`
  next to the snippets you actually reuse.
