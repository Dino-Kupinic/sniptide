# Acceptable-use policy draft

Status: proposed for review under WRK-329. Publication requires the [activation checklist](README.md#activation-checklist), an effective date, an identified operator, and working report and appeal contacts.

## Scope

Sniptide is for sharing code and text. These rules apply to paste content, titles, descriptions, filenames, tags, revisions, and other material you submit. They apply across the website, API, CLI, and MCP, regardless of visibility, passwords, expiry, or one-time settings.

Links, encoded payloads, archives expressed as text, and instructions used to distribute prohibited material fall under the same rules. Moving material to a private paste or splitting it into multiple pastes does not exempt it.

## Prohibited material

Do not use Sniptide to create, store, distribute, solicit, advertise, or facilitate access to any of the following:

- Child sexual abuse or exploitation, sexualization of minors, grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, or sexual extortion involving minors.
- Sexual content depicting or describing minors, including fictional, simulated, and AI-generated content. This is a service rule regardless of whether a particular depiction meets a jurisdiction's definition of illegal material.
- Adult pornography, explicit sexual narratives intended for arousal, or promotion of sexual services.
- Non-consensual intimate material, sexual coercion, or instructions or links used to obtain or distribute such material.
- Gratuitous graphic depictions or descriptions of severe injury, torture, mutilation, or death intended to shock, celebrate cruelty, or entertain through gore.
- Attempts to evade a moderation restriction or help others access material removed under these rules.

## Contextual material

Non-exploitative medical information, sex education, abuse reporting, journalism, research, and ordinary technical discussion may be allowed when the context justifies the content. A reference to sex, violence, or abuse does not alone establish a violation.

These contexts do not permit sharing child sexual abuse material, exploitative sexual depictions of minors, non-consensual intimate material, or links intended to provide access to them. Describe a concern without reproducing the prohibited material. Use a secure reporting channel for its location and the minimum information necessary.

## Reports and decisions

Report a suspected violation through the operator's published abuse channel. For suspected child exploitation, provide the paste URL and a short explanation. Do not download material, attach copies, or repost it to make a report.

Sniptide may restrict access while a report is assessed, remove content, limit sharing, or suspend an account. A moderation decision applies across access methods, including owner access. Automated signals may assist review but do not establish that a crime occurred.

The operator provides a reason and an appeal route where appropriate and legally permitted. A successful appeal can restore content only when ordinary access conditions also permit it. Reporting volume alone does not establish a violation.

Material and related records may be preserved when there is an applicable legal basis or obligation. Retention, reporting, disclosure, and disposal follow the operator's confirmed procedure and published privacy information. A removal decision does not necessarily mean every copy can immediately be erased.
