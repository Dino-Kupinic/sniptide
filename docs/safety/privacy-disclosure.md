# Moderation privacy disclosure draft

Status: proposed wording for review under WRK-329. Adapt it to the actual operator, deployed controls, service providers, and applicable law before publication. This document does not replace Sniptide's full privacy policy.

## Purpose and scope

Sniptide handles abuse reports and moderation records to enforce its content rules, respond to safety incidents, and meet applicable legal obligations. These activities can concern public, unlisted, private, and password-protected pastes. Visibility and password settings control user access. They do not provide end-to-end encryption or make content unreadable to the operator.

## Information involved

Depending on the report and the authorized response, moderation can involve paste identifiers, URLs, content versions, account identifiers, report details, decision reasons, timestamps, appeal records, and reporting references. Authorized responders may need access to paste content or existing account and request metadata.

The operator collects only what is necessary for the case and its confirmed obligations. Routine logs, notifications, and analytics must not contain paste bodies, passwords, API tokens, environment secrets, or copies of suspected exploitative material.

## Access and external services

Only authorized personnel and appropriately contracted service providers may access moderation records for the documented purpose. Disclosures to authorities follow the applicable legal process and reporting requirements.

These drafts do not introduce an external classifier. Before enabling one, the operator must assess its legal basis, data-processing terms, retention, subprocessors, locations, transfers, and treatment of private code and secrets. Publish the actual provider and processing details before use. Do not promise that all material remains on Sniptide's servers if external processing is enabled.

## Retention and deletion

Ordinary paste deletion and moderation-record retention are different operations. Content or related records may need preservation under a confirmed legal obligation or another established legal basis. Record the scope and duration of each authorized hold. Restrict preserved material from ordinary user access.

Define retention schedules for reports, decisions, appeals, access records, and backups. Review holds and dispose of records when their authorized purpose ends. Do not promise immediate erasure from backups or unrestricted deletion of preserved material. Do not retain all reports or content indefinitely.

## Before publication

Supply the operator's identity, privacy contact, abuse and appeal contacts, legal bases, applicable rights and request process, retention schedules, service providers, international-transfer information, and authority-disclosure procedure. Confirm that the published wording matches the deployed service and the full privacy policy.
